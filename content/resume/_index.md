## Research Experience

### Robotic Modeling, Control & Estimation

> Period: 2019-07 / 2024-12

- Applied energy-based control algorithms and developed a muscular model to simulate octopus arms, using Cosserat rods as a fundamental framework. Demonstrated the versatility of these algorithms through a range of manipulations, including dynamic movements such as reaching, fetching, and grasping, contributing to a deeper understanding of biomechanics and soft robotics.
- Expanded the application of energy-based control and estimation algorithms to physical soft actuators incorporating pneumatic artificial muscles. This work demonstrated the potential for real-world applications in soft robotics and biomechanics, advancing flexible and adaptable robotic systems.

### Online Sound Wave Learning and Detection

> Period: 2023-08 / 2024-05

- Implemented an online frequency transform algorithm by integrating a Kalman-based filter. This real-time algorithm enabled continuous monitoring of sound waves, dynamically adapting to changing frequencies and providing efficient online analysis.
- Used the duality of nonlinear filters to improve the identification of optimal control parameters. This approach enabled online learning, detection, and recognition of intricate frequency patterns associated with musical notes, improving accuracy and adaptability in online sound wave analysis.
